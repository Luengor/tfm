## ⚠️ Headline: the fixed embedding was swapped underneath the experiment

`report.md` documents the embedding as **DINOv2 style head, 384-d**. The new
run uses the **retrained style head at 128-d** (verified in the DBs: 512-byte
blobs = 128×float32). Cause is commit `e6c4fcd "style retrain"` (2026-06-02),
which rewrote the head training (`style_trainer.py` deleted, `style_head.py`
added), retrained weights (`dinov2_graffiti_style_head.pth` mtime 2026-06-02),
and dropped the output dim 384→128 in `embeddings.py`.

Consequence: **old↔new per-run diffs conflate the reduction axis with an
embedding change** — they are not a clean comparison. The new run is still a
valid `reduction × corpus` sweep, but **on a different embedding**. Decision
needed before editing the report:

- Intended new baseline → rewrite `report.md` for the 128-d embedding (numbers
  and the "identity collapse" evidence below are all stale).
- Accidental pickup of the retrained head → re-run §2 on the original 384-d
  embedding.

Several other commits in the window touch the pipeline (`9a19e4b deterministic`
added seeds to `embeddings.py`/`umap.py`; normalization/terminology commits), so
the embedding change cannot be cleanly attributed to dimensionality alone.

## What changed vs the report (and what didn't)

**UMAP-10 verdict survives.** Applying the report's own joint criterion
(non-trivial k + moderate noise + balanced csCV) to the new 6416 cell, UMAP-10
is again the *only* reduction that passes: k=562 ✓, noise 0.20 ✓, csCV 0.74 ✓.
Identity (0.68), pca-50 (0.70), isomap (0.62) all fail on noise; pca-10
collapses to k=2. UMAP-10 keeps moderate noise (0.06→0.11→0.20) and the most
balanced clusters (0.63→0.55→0.74) across all three corpus sizes, exactly as
before. **Recommendation = UMAP, n_components=10, unchanged.**

**The "identity collapses → reduction is mandatory" evidence is stale.** On
384-d, identity gave k≤4 everywhere and clustered 70× slower (24.1 s @6416).
On the 128-d embedding, identity gives k=52 @1000 and **k=301 @6416** (sil 0.307,
highest in group) and clusters in **6.8 s** (was 24.1 s). Identity no longer
*collapses* — instead it fails the joint criterion by **high noise (0.60–0.68)**,
clustering only a dense core and rejecting most points. The conclusion
("UMAP-10 default") holds, but its *justification* must change from "identity
collapses to k≤4" to "identity/pca-50 over-reject (high noise, unbalanced)".

**The silhouette/CH/DB inversion hazard still real, but relocated.** Previously
identity/pca-50/isomap won silhouette via the k=2 trap. Now identity (0.307) and
pca-50 (0.238) post the *highest* silhouettes @6416 while sitting at ~0.68 noise —
same trap, different cells. The "never read silhouette alone" warning stands.

**PCA still not robust:** pca-10 degenerates (k=2, sil≈0 @6416; **negative sil
−0.020 @1000**); pca-50 swings to the high-noise/unbalanced regime (k=238, noise
0.70, csCV 2.29 @6416). **Isomap still not robust**, in a *new* failure shape:
now k=2 collapse @1000 and k=125/noise 0.62/csCV 3.4 @6416, still the most
expensive stage (23.9 s @6416). Both still reinforce, not refute, UMAP.

**UMAP-10 ≈ UMAP-50** still holds (quality-indistinguishable, UMAP-50 ~2× cost).

## Bottom line

- The §2 *recommendation* (UMAP n_components=10) is intact.
- Every *number* in `report.md`, the **384-d premise**, and the entire
  "identity collapses to k≤4 / 70× slower" argument are stale and must be
  rewritten for the 128-d embedding — *after* confirming the swap was intended.
- `report.md` left untouched pending that decision.
